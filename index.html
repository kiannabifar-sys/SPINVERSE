01 / THE BIG PICTURE

SMALL BALL.
BIG ENERGY.

Table tennis, also known as ping-pong, is a fast-paced racket sport played by two opponents in singles or four players in doubles.

Players use small rackets to hit a lightweight ball over a net. The goal is simple: make a legal return that your opponent cannot successfully return.

But mastering the game takes much more than hitting the ball. Players need excellent footwork, quick reactions, concentration, and tactical thinking.

02 / FROM THE PAST TO THE PRESENT

A HISTORY OF
SPEED.

From a home pastime to a global competitive sport, table tennis has travelled a remarkable journey.

1880s

The Beginning

Early versions of table tennis developed in England as an indoor game inspired by lawn tennis.

1926

International Sport

The International Table Tennis Federation (ITTF) was founded, and the first World Championships took place.

1988

Olympic Stage

Table tennis became an official Olympic sport at the Seoul Olympic Games.

TODAY

Global Competition

Millions enjoy the sport, from recreational players to elite athletes competing internationally.

03 / KNOW THE GAME

THE RULES
OF PLAY.

Learn the fundamentals before stepping up to the table. These basic rules make the game fair and exciting.

01
🎯

Scoring

A game is normally played to 11 points. You must win by at least two points. At 10–10, play continues until one player leads by two.

02
🏓

The Serve

The ball starts on an open palm, is thrown nearly vertically at least 16 cm, and is struck so it bounces on the server's side and then the receiver's side.

03
🔄

Alternating Serves

Players normally serve two points each in turn. After 10–10, service alternates after every point.

04
🧠

Winning a Point

You score when your opponent fails to make a legal return, commits a fault, or sends the ball out of play.

05
🤝

Doubles

In doubles, partners alternate hitting the ball. The serve must travel diagonally from the right half of the server's court to the right half of the receiver's court.

06
🏆

Matches

A match consists of an odd number of games. The winner is the player or pair that wins the required majority of games.

Educational summary. Official regulations are published by the International Table Tennis Federation (ITTF).

04 / BUILD YOUR GAME

MASTER THE
TECHNIQUES.

Every great player combines reliable basic strokes with spin, placement, and smart movement.

TECHNIQUE 01

THE FOREHAND

The forehand is a powerful attacking stroke played on the racket-hand side of your body. It is useful for creating speed, applying pressure, and finishing opportunities.

COACH'S TIP:
Stay balanced, rotate your body naturally, and make contact in front of you. Prioritize control before adding power.

06 / WHAT YOU NEED

THE EQUIPMENT
LAB.

The right equipment helps you develop good technique, consistent control, and confidence.

🏓

The Racket

A wooden blade covered with approved rubber sheets. Grip and blade feel affect control and speed.

⚪

The Ball

A lightweight, hollow plastic ball with a standard diameter of 40 mm in modern competition.

📐

The Table

A regulation table is 2.74 m long and 1.525 m wide, with its playing surface 76 cm above the floor.

👟

Sports Shoes

Indoor shoes with suitable grip and support help players move quickly and maintain balance.
